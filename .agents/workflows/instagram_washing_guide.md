# KULT Editorial: Instagram Content Washing Guideline

This document defines the rules for transforming raw, marketing-heavy Instagram captions into KULT's premium, minimalist, and poetic editorial articles. Use these rules whenever importing new content from an Instagram link.

---

## 1. Tone of Voice (어조와 분위기)

- **Style**: Literary, minimalist, and atmospheric (e.g., Magazine B, Kinfolk, or Hypebeast).
- **Korean Tone**: Polite, formal, and reflective (해요체 대신 격식 있는 명사형 종결이나 하십시오체/해라체 위주의 에디토리얼 어조 사용).
  - *Bad*: "오늘 성수동 나스 팝업에 다녀왔어요! 🌸 너무 이쁜 틴트와 사은품이 많으니 다들 꼭 가보세요! 링크는 프로필에!"
  - *Good*: "성수동의 콘크리트 골목 속, 나스(NARS)가 제안하는 새로운 빛과 색의 레이어. 감각적인 텍스처와 독창적인 전시 구성을 통해 뷰티를 하나의 시각 예술로 재정의한다."
- **English Tone**: Sophisticated, conceptual, and descriptive.
  - *Bad*: "Nars new pop-up in Seongsu is open now! Check out the cute lip tints and free gifts. Link in bio!"
  - *Good*: "An atmospheric exploration of light and pigment. Nars introduces its latest collection within a Brutalist concrete shell in Seongsu-dong, transforming cosmetic trial into a spatial narrative."

---

## 2. Text Washing Rules (텍스트 정제 규칙)

### 🚫 Strip Social Noise (소셜 노이즈 제거)
- **Hashtags**: Remove all hashtags (e.g. `#nars #seongsu #kbeauty`).
- **Marketing Buzzwords**: Remove direct promotional text, price discounts, or urgency-driving phrases (e.g., "20% OFF", "Limited time!", "Don't miss out!").
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Remove social-media specific CTAs (e.g., "Link in bio", "Follow us", "Leave a comment").
- **Emojis**: Remove excessive emojis. Allow maximum 1-2 subtle emojis per section only if they add aesthetic value (e.g., 🌿, ☕). Never use screaming or multiple emojis (e.g., 🚨🚨🚨, 👍👍).

### ✍️ Transcreation (초월 번역)
- Do not translate literally. Write the English copy from scratch based on the concept, or translate the Korean text into English using premium, design-oriented vocabulary (e.g., replacing "pretty design" with "minimalist geometry", "nice smell" with "olfactory landscape").

---

## 3. Title & Subtitle Generation (제목 및 소제목 규격)

- **Main Title**: Must be conceptual and focus on the spatial/aesthetic experience rather than just the brand name.
  - *Format*: `[Theme/Concept]: [Spatial/Brand Subtitle]`
  - *Example*: `The Geometry of Scent: Tamburins Sinsa-dong`
- **Section Titles**: Should reflect a narrative flow (e.g., chronological "11:00 AM - Morning Bloom" or thematic "Feature 1: Uncommon Laboratory").

---

## 4. Metadata Mapping (메타데이터 매핑)

- **Category**: Map to one of the KULT standard categories: `Architecture`, `Wellness`, `Retail / Beauty`, `Heritage`, `Healing Stay`.
- **Region**: Map to lowercase regional slugs (e.g., `seongsu`, `sinsa`, `hannam`, `bukchon`, `gyeongju`).
- **Premium Status**: Define if the column requires credits to unlock.
